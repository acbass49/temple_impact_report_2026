Key challenges and decisions made during analysis:
- The biggest challenge was that before 2010 Zillow data was missing for many zipcodes.
Because of this, I had to limit my analysis to temples that were dedicated after 2010.
- The monthly Zillow data was very noisy, so I aggregated to quarterly data to smooth out some of the noise.
- I included a number of control variables to help isolate the effect of temple dedications on home values.
- I decided to use announcement date as vibes and speculation often drive economic processes. In future 
    analyses, I would like to compare announcement date effects to dedication date effects or
    ground breaking effects.
